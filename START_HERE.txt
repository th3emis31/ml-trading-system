# JARVIS - 5 SIMPLE STEPS

## STEP 1: Open Browser
- Look for Chrome, Firefox, or Edge icon on desktop
- Double-click it
- Wait for browser to open

## STEP 2: Copy This Address
```
http://127.0.0.1:5001/jarvis-voice
```

## STEP 3: Paste Address in Browser
1. Click in the address bar (top of browser)
2. Paste the address above
3. Press ENTER key
4. Wait 5 seconds for page to load

## STEP 4: Click ALLOW for Microphone
- Browser will ask permission
- Click BLUE "ALLOW" button
- Microphone is now ready

## STEP 5: Use Voice
1. Click the big MICROPHONE circle
2. Say: **"Hey JARVIS, hello"**
3. JARVIS will respond
4. Done!
